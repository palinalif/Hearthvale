# M1 physical Thor playtest

**Not run on this build.** Earlier M0 feedback does not establish M1 performance or controller comfort. Use the M1 APK linked in the milestone report; install as an update, keeping app data. Record the displayed renderer, build version, display refresh rate and device performance mode.

1. At normal distance and close up, judge the same cottage and riverbank: terrain step size, cottage proportions, trees, colours, sunlight and water. Check that the target highlight, ghost and resize handles are clear without covering the work.
2. Using only the controller, move one of the six windows, replace another, suppress another and add a flower box. Widen and shorten the cottage, change its height and material, then undo and redo. Check the manual choices survive and a cancelled resize changes nothing.
3. Move an attachment near an edge, shrink past it, then recover it onto a valid surface. Delete a supporting surface and recover its detail. Duplicate the cottage; editing the copy must leave the original unchanged.
4. Switch to terrain. Hold raise and dig still, then drag connected strokes. Extend a level pad at a fixed sampled height, retain the height for another stroke, resample, extend a slope and smooth the bank. Excavate a wall and inspect the remaining overhang. Adjust radius, strength, falloff and precision without touch.
5. Cancel a changed stroke; undo and redo a committed stroke. Open tools or pause while holding A, and test focus loss and controller disconnection where available. Editing must stop and remain stopped until a fresh press.
6. Save the edited terrain and cottage, quit fully and relaunch. Check all manual choices, suppression, recoverable details and terrain remain. Repeat after a second save.
7. Play and sculpt for at least ten minutes. Record FPS/frame pacing at rest and while editing, any pauses during save/resize, heat and throttling, and whether targets or menu labels are difficult to read. Record unavailable memory or mesh-completion measurements as unavailable.

Report failures with the action sequence and a screenshot/video when practical. The highest-value feedback is whether you can predict the affected terrain and finish the cottage workflow comfortably. M1 remains open until the required physical checks and visual review are recorded. No catalogue expansion follows this build.
